Content

Home page

Homepage for Secret Life of Ryan acts as a hub for the website, for the viewer to understand how the website works, and what the user can expect. Introduction portion: The introduction section will serve as a spot to describe a little about myself, also to offer the audience an idea at my style, with some photos from travels, that will also help to serve as visual aid for my love for travel to help create credibility on the subject. This will help to let the audience become familar with me. Dashboard: The dashboard section will inlcude features for the rest of the site, including the U.S. Travel and Global travel pages, and explanations for both so the user can navigate which site they would be interesed in. Ex: "Thinking about your next roadtrip across the country? Here are some spots that may help to serve as the beginning of your adventure!" This portion would remain the same for the Global travel section, too, but with a broader explanation to leave room for the audience to see for themselves, "Whatever your travel needs may be, the world is your sandbox! check out the Global Travel page to see what country is your next destination!"

Images for the Home page

U.S. Travel

U.S Travel page will go more into depth about destinations and attractions within the United States. For the site to not go into great detail, there will be spots featured for categories such as the weather, tailored around the best attractions to go to and what best time of the year to visit, with hints at what would best fit the users interest. "Monument Valley is one of the best wonders of the country, with a step into the past that takes you into one of the great deserts of America, but do not be alarmed, for the right time of year could shape how you view this experience. If you are looking for true desert vibes, July will offer a scorching step into what was reality for many people, with temperatures rising to 100 degrees fahrenheit! December offers a colder experience, with temperatures reaching 44 degrees fahrenheit!" Introductions like these under the showcase will help to paint a picture for the audience to see what destination might sound great, and the time of year will offer information that will help make their decision. The bottom of the page will offer "Hot Spots" for some of the busiest destinations in America, such as "Yellowstone," and "New York City." These spots will also include average tourist visitors per year. Lastly, there will be a "recommendation from a friend" portion, showcasing a destination recommendation from one of my friends, a reliable source that offers a friendly approach to gain information on a destination in the United States.

Images for U.S. Travel Page

Global Travel

Global Travel, will include similar to that of the U.S. travel page, but with a focus less on the United States and more on the entire globe. The page will introduce the viewer with an idea of "195 countries, one click away! This page will offer more spots tailored to offering the audience a better understanding at what travel may fit them, with pictures from my global travels, with location and descriptions included. Category section will be serve as the spot where viewers will be able to read more about what country is the right one for them. Categories will differ by continent, and the United States will still be included, but won't be the center focus. By continent, countries will be categorized by weather and most popular times of year. "Hot Spots" will act as a generalization of the most popular spots in the world, no categories or specifics. Will be followed with most popular time of the year and least popular. "Paris: Busiest Month: July, slowest month: February, yearly tourists: 37 Million." This page will also offer a budget friendly section, with spots that are more common to have deals and focuses on pure convenience. "Indonesia: Average traveler budget daily: $50."
