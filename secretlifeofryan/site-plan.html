Content

Home page

This homepage is made to share with others a little bit about me, and my travels in my life. Although I am still working on my travels and exploring the world, I still have some cool spots I want to share with everybody. The homepage will help to serve as the all in one for the website, giving the site visitor everything they need to know about what I have to offer. Some of the images I will implement will also help to serve as a visual aid, providing a few photos from my personal travels with everyone, to see where I have been, and also where I want to go. As the Home will act as a hub, I also want to be able to offer written explanations for the travels, that will help to let others know more about my life. It would also be nice to include a dashboard, being able to capture a highlight or a travel destination and include some tips on that destination, outside of the Tips page. Lastly, I want to include a section that shares information about my friends, that may offer a showcase on a favorite destination of theirs and why!

Images for the Home page

U.S. Travel

The Travels page of the website will offer a deeper insight into the category of travels specifically within the United States, including travels of my own throughout the country. With this page, I want to offer insight into others who are also interested in traveling the country, and want to be able to find out more, in an easy and enlightening environment! This page will offer an idea on what travels may be for them, based on what they might be looking for, with some travel showcases and information about that destination under the showcase! I want to be able to offer help for travels within the United States, as well as travel information for Internatinal travels, which will be included in the seocnd website page. This site will include a recommendation from a friend, as well as some spots that will be tailored for specific people looking for something specific, or different! I want to be able to include a few more photos, along with the countries or areas I will be describing, to include a visual experience for the users. Lastly, I want to include a section of the page for hot spots in the country, that will be aside from what people may be looking for, in hopes to give the site visitors something new to see, or inspiration for a future travel!

Images for U.S. Travel Page

Global Travel

The Global Travel page will offer the same as the U.S. Travel page, but tailored specifically for traveling the globe. This page will still have a United states information, but will not be the center focus of the page, and will be specifically focusing on other countries and continents. Similar to U.S. travels, there will be some cool showcases from a friend, as well as cateogories that will make it easier for people looking for specific spots and ideas! I will be including countries on a seperate part of the page that will showcase Engligh speaking countries, and other languages that would be considered useful for learning if you are planning to travel to other countries that also may share the same language. I am going to implement again a part of the page that offers insight on hot spots around the globe, as well as what is popular around certain times of the year. Lastly, I am going to include a section for what would be some cheap budget-friendly countries, as well as couuntries that may not be ideal for those who are looking to save money. All of these features I believe will create a unique user experience for those wanting to learn more about what destination may be their next!
